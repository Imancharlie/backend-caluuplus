45. Performance Requirements

The academic calendar may contain many events.

The timeline should remain smooth.

Avoid:

unnecessary re-renders
per-second React state updates
expensive calculations on every render
rebuilding every event unnecessarily

Memoize derived calculations where appropriate.

Use a virtualized horizontal list if the number of events becomes large.

For a normal academic year, however, the number of events is likely small enough that a normal horizontal list may be sufficient.

Do not over-engineer before necessary.
